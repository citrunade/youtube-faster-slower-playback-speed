# Privacy policy: YouTube Faster / Slower Control

Last updated: 16 November 2026

YouTube Faster / Slower Control ("the extension") changes the playback speed and pitch of videos on YouTube in your own browser, and holds the picture back slightly so it stays in sync with the processed sound. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

## What the extension stores

Only your settings (speed, pitch, tape mode, sound tuning, picture sync and its fine-tune, bass, drum tail, reverb), in the browser's extension storage on your own computer. For the Auto sound tuning, the extension reads the current video's YouTube category and title in the page; this is kept in memory for that tab only, never saved and never sent anywhere.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any other server.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not sell or share data with anyone.
- It does not record or save audio or video. The video's sound and picture are processed live, as they play, and then discarded.
- It does not keep any history of what you watch.

## Permissions

- **Storage**: to remember your settings on your computer.
- **Access to youtube.com (www, m and music)**: to set the playback speed on the video, pass the video's sound through the pitch correction and effects (bass, reverb, drum tail) inside the page so it plays sped up or slowed down at the original pitch, and show the slightly delayed picture that keeps it in sync with the sound. The extension does not run on any other site.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.
